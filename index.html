How it works

Six steps, no human in the loop until it matters

01

Capture

Portal enquiry emails, Facebook and Instagram lead ads, and your website form all land in one list, in one shape.

02

Read and score

The system pulls out district, budget, property type and timeline from how the buyer actually wrote it, then sorts the lead into hot, warm or cold. Investors and overseas buyers are flagged separately.

03

Match from your inventory

It selects properties from your own list that fit the district and budget. Nothing outside that list is ever mentioned.

04

Reply and assign

The buyer gets a useful answer on WhatsApp. At the same moment the agent who covers that district gets the full lead: name, number, budget, score.

05

Book the visit

For hot leads it finds a free slot in your calendar and confirms it, inside working hours and around the local weekend and prayer times.

06

Keep following up

Day 2, 5, 10 and 21, each time with a different message. The sequence stops the moment the buyer replies, books, or asks to stop.

Limits, on purpose

What this system will not do

Each of these is a decision. Read them before you buy, not after.

✕ It does not quote prices

Asked for a price, it offers a viewing and hands the question to your agent. An AI that states the wrong price confidently costs far more than one that stays quiet.

✕ It does not invent properties

It speaks only from your inventory sheet. No project, payment plan or availability that you have not entered.

✕ It does not close deals

It answers, qualifies and delivers a booked meeting. Showing the property and negotiating stays with your team. This replaces the waiting, not the salesperson.

✕ It does not harvest contacts

No scraping and no purchased lists. Every lead comes from your own channels or from someone who contacted you first.

On messaging rules. WhatsApp Business permits messages only to people who have contacted you, and the first message must use an approved template. We follow that exactly, because the number is the most valuable thing in the whole setup and it is lost permanently when those rules are broken.
